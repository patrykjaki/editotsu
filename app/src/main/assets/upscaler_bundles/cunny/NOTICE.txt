These files are the complete source of the shaders. Editotsu loads them
into libmpv through the glsl-shaders option. On OpenGL ES (vo=gpu) the
non-dp4a files are loaded from a copy in which the optional float16
#extension line is commented out, because GLES rejects #extension after
other code; the bundled files themselves stay byte-identical to upstream.
